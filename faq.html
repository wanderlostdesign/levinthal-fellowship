The program

What is the Levinthal Fellowship?

It's a venture capital and entrepreneurship fellowship for BYU students, hosted by venture capitalist Mike Levinthal. Fellows take a Winter semester seminar, work remotely with a Mexico City startup during the semester, and then spend a month in Mexico City working at that company in person.

How is it related to the Mayfield Fellows Program?

Mike was an early champion of Stanford's Mayfield Fellows Program and brought Mayfield Fund on board as its sponsor. That program pairs a small cohort with venture coursework and a startup internship. The Levinthal Fellowship follows the same model at BYU, with the internship in Mexico City. Read more about Mike.

What is the seminar like?

It's a discussion seminar taught in the style of Harvard Business School's case method, and there are no lectures. You read the assigned material ahead of time, come ready to take a position, and work through real decisions with the group. Founders and investors from Mike's network often join as guests.

The seminar meets on Tuesday evenings during Winter semester 2027. Some sessions are held at Mike's home in Park City.

Do I get course credit?

Yes. The fellowship counts as a 3-credit course.

What kinds of companies will I work with?

You'll be placed at a tech startup in Mexico City. The application asks which areas interest you most, including SaaS, fintech, social entrepreneurship, food & beverage, healthcare, edtech, consumer goods, sustainability, and nonprofit & impact ventures. We use your choices to find a good match.

Time commitment

How much time does it take during the semester?

Plan on the Tuesday evening seminar plus its readings, and about 5 hours a week of remote work with your startup.

When is the Mexico City internship?

The in-person internship runs April 25 – May 25, 2027, right after Winter semester ends. You'll spend the full month in Mexico City working on-site with your company.

What if I can't commit to the full month?

Please don't apply this year. The in-person month is the core of the fellowship, and every fellow needs to be there for the whole internship.

I already have a spring or summer internship. Can I still apply?

The application asks about any upcoming internships that aren't on your résumé. Be upfront about them. What matters is whether you can be in Mexico City for the full April 25 – May 25, 2027 window.

Eligibility

Who can apply?

BYU students from any major. You don't need to be studying business or computer science. We're looking for curious, driven people who care about solving real problems.

Do I need to speak Spanish?

No. Spanish is helpful and the application asks about it, but it isn't required.

Do I need startup or finance experience?

No. Many fellows will be learning about venture for the first time. The application cares more about how you think: a problem you're passionate about, what you've done about it, and why you want to be part of this.

Applying

What's on the application?

Basic contact information, your major, a Google Drive link to your résumé, and a few yes/no questions about availability and Spanish. Then four short responses:

  • A problem you're passionate about solving, and what you've done about it
  • Your dream career, in a sentence or two
  • The most important problem facing young people today, and why
  • Why you're interested in the fellowship and what you hope to accomplish
Any tips for the résumé link?

Make sure the sharing settings on your Google Drive file are set to "Anyone with the link can view." If we can't open it, we can't review it.

What's the deadline, and what happens next?
